ARCHITECTURE

The Transformer at a glance

ENCODER

6 identical layers

Multi-head self-attention followed by a feed-forward network.

DECODER

6 layers + encoder attention

Adds a third sub-layer that attends over the encoder output.

EVERY SUB-LAYER

Residual + layer norm

Residual connections and layer normalization around each sub-layer.
