ORDER INFORMATION

Positional encoding

THE PROBLEM

No sense of order

Without recurrence or convolution, the model cannot tell where a word sits in the sentence.

THE FIX

Sine and cosine signals

Waves of different frequencies are added to the word embeddings, so relative positions are easy to learn.

Learned position embeddings perform nearly the same
