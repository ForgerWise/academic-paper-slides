Why recurrence holds models back

01

Sequential by design

Each hidden state depends on the previous one, so a sequence cannot be processed in parallel.

02

Long-range dependencies

Signals between distant words must travel through many steps, which makes them hard to learn.

03

Costly training

At longer sequence lengths, memory limits batching and training becomes slow.

Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need, NIPS 2017
