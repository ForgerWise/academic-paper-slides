[≤ 9:15] Now the main result. The table shows BLEU scores for both tasks. The first two rows are the best earlier systems, and both are ensembles of several models. The last two rows are the Transformer. On English to German, the big Transformer reaches 28.4. That is more than two points above the best ensemble. Even the base model is ahead of every earlier system. On English to French, the big model reaches 41.8, which was a new record for a single model. The base model is weaker on this task.
