Conclusion

  • Background: recurrent models, attention only as a helper
  • Problem: slow training, weak over long distances
  • Solution: the Transformer, built on attention alone
  • Results
  • English–German: 28.4 BLEU, 2 above any earlier result
  • Training cost a fraction of earlier models
  • Also works for parsing

Source: Vaswani et al., NIPS 2017, Sec. 6 and 7

[≤ 13:08] Let me summarize. Translation models were recurrent networks, and attention was only a helper. Recurrence made them slow to train and weak over long distances. The Transformer is the first translation model built on attention alone. In the experiments it set a new best score on both translation tasks, and it trained at a fraction of the cost of earlier models. At the end of the paper the authors say they want to apply the model to other kinds of data, such as images, audio and video. Thank you for listening.
