Model Variations

  • One setting of the base model changed at a time
  • Too few or too many heads score lower; a bigger model scores higher
  • Dropout is very helpful: 1.2 BLEU lower without it
Change to the base modelBLEU (English–German, dev)

1 head24.9

Table: Vaswani et al., NIPS 2017, Table 3 (five of twenty rows: base, A, C, D), Sec. 6.2

[≤ 11:36] Which parts of the model matter? The authors changed one setting at a time and measured BLEU on the development set. The first row is the base model, with 25.8. Look at the heads first. One head is clearly worse than eight, and thirty-two heads is also worse. A larger feed-forward layer scores higher, so a bigger model helps. Next, dropout. Without dropout the score falls by more than one point.
