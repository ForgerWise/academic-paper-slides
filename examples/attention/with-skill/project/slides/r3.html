[≤ 11:36] Which parts of the model matter? The authors changed one setting at a time and measured BLEU on the development set. The first row is the base model, with 25.8. Now look at the heads. One head is clearly worse than eight, but thirty-two heads is also worse, so more is not always better. Next, dropout. Without dropout the score falls by more than one point, the largest drop in this table. Last, the position vectors. A learned table gives almost the same score as the sine and cosine waves. So the choice of waves is not what makes the model work.
