[≤ 12:28] Does the model only work for translation? The authors tested a very different task, English constituency parsing. Here the output is a tree structure and it is much longer than the input. They used a Transformer with four layers and did very little tuning. Trained only on the Wall Street Journal data, it reaches an F1 score of 91.3, a little under the best earlier result. With extra semi-supervised data it reaches 92.7, above the earlier semi-supervised results. So the architecture carries over to another task.
