[≤ 9:18] Here is how the experiments were run. There are two translation tasks from WMT 2014. English to German has about four and a half million sentence pairs. English to French is much larger, with thirty-six million sentences. Training used one machine with eight GPUs. There are two model sizes. The base model trained for twelve hours, and the big model trained for three and a half days. The models were tested on the newstest 2014 set and scored with BLEU, where higher is better. The results are compared with the best recurrent and convolutional models at that time.
