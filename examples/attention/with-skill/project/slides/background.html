[≤ 0:25] Let me start with the background. In 2017 machine translation was done with an encoder and a decoder, the two boxes in the middle of the diagram. The encoder reads the input sentence and turns it into a list of vectors. The decoder then writes the translation one word at a time. Both parts were recurrent networks. The best systems also added attention, the red dashed arrow. It lets the decoder look back at any word of the input. So attention already existed, but only as a helper for the recurrent network.
