[≤ 5:38] The other stack is the decoder, and the diagram shows one of its layers. It also has six identical layers, but each layer has three parts. The first part is self-attention over the words written so far. It is masked, so a word can only look at the words before it. The second part attends over the output of the encoder. This is how the decoder reads the input sentence. The third part is the same feed-forward network as in the encoder. At the top, a linear layer and a softmax turn the result into a probability for every possible next word.
