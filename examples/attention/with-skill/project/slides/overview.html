Transformer Components

The Transformer is an encoder and a decoder built from attention and feed-forward layers only.

Input

source sentence

Encoder and Decoder Stacks

Six layers each, no recurrence

Output

translation

Attention

Relates all words
in every layer

Positional Encoding

Adds the word order
to the input

Source: Vaswani et al., NIPS 2017, Sec. 3; diagram drawn by presenter

[≤ 4:06] So here is the authors' answer. They call the model the Transformer. It keeps the encoder and decoder, but everything inside is built from attention and small feed-forward layers. A sentence goes in on the left and its translation comes out on the right. I will explain the model in three parts. First come the encoder and decoder stacks. Then attention, which works inside every layer of both stacks. Last comes positional encoding, which gives the stacks the order of the words.
