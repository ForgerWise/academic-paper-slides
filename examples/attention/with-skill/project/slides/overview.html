[≤ 4:00] So here is the authors' answer. They call the model the Transformer. It keeps the encoder and decoder structure, but inside there is no recurrence and no convolution. Everything is built from attention and small feed-forward layers. The figure shows the whole model, and I will explain it in four parts. First, the encoder and decoder stacks. Second, the attention function at the core. Third, how several attention functions are combined into heads. And fourth, how the model learns word order.
