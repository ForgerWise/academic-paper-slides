Source: Vaswani et al., NIPS 2017, Sec. 1 and 4; diagram drawn by presenter

[≤ 1:12] This design has two problems, and both come from recurrence. A recurrent network reads a sentence one word at a time, like the chain on the slide. Every step needs the result of the step before it. So the words of one sentence cannot be processed in parallel, and training on long sentences is slow. The second problem is distance. When two related words are far apart, the signal between them has to pass through every step in between, and that makes the relation hard to learn. So the authors set one goal. They want to model a sequence with attention only, and with no recurrence at all.
