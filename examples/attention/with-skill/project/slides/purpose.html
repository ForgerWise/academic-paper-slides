[≤ 2:58] So here is the research question. Can attention alone replace recurrence and convolution in a translation model? Earlier models used attention too, but only as an addition to a recurrent network. The authors remove the recurrent part completely. They have two aims. The first is quality, so the translations should be at least as good as before. The second is speed. If the model has no step by step dependency, the whole sentence can be computed in parallel, and training should take much less time.
