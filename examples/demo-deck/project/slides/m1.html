Method 1

  • Learn a general map from surveyed buildings
  • Input: floor plan and access point positions
  • Output: predicted signal strength per location
  • Adapt it to the new building
  • Uses the new floor plan only, no survey

Figure: [Author et al., Venue Year, Fig. 2]. Demo content, not a real paper.

[≤ 1:04] Here is the first half of the method. The authors train one model on buildings that were already measured. The model gets two things as input. It gets the floor plan, and it gets the positions of the access points. From these it predicts how strong the signal is at every location. Why does this help? Because the model learns how walls weaken a signal in general, not in one building. Then comes the new building. The model only needs its floor plan. Nobody has to walk around with a phone.
